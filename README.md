# A Novel Approach to Studying the Role Influence Plays in Team Collective Intelligence

by Lisa R. O'Bryan, T. Oxendahl, S. Garnier, Santiago Segarra, W. Wettergreen, A. Sabharwal, and M. E. Beier.

This code replicates the data processing steps and analyses presented in *Collective Intelligence*, 4(2) ([view article](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/26339137251343584)).

## Summary

In this paper, we present an objective measure of individual influence over team decision-making during a virtual engineering design challenge. In our study, participants used the platform [Line Rider](https://www.linerider.com/) to solve a virtual design challenge with measurable performance outcomes. Solutions take the form of 2D tracks through an obstacle course. Participants first created individual-level solutions independently. They then worked together to design a single group-level solution. We measure influence as the objective similarity between the team-level solution and each individual's initial individual-level design. The code presented here enables the processing of 2D tracks extracted from the Line Rider platform, the calculation of our objective influence measure, and analyses to determine the association between individuals' influence measures and their characteristics such as personality, communication behavior, and idea quality. We also examine the association between the characteristics of a team's most influential member and the team's collective intelligence.

## Software implementation

All R scripts for data processing and statistical analyses are located in the `R Code` folder, and the corresponding datasets are located in the `Data` folder.
